Sayak — Privacy Policy

Published by Zero Double Two. Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Sayak is a single-player archery game. This page explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we do not collect

The game has no accounts. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location, and it records no video or audio of you. It does not use your device's advertising ID.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (levels cleared, diyas, coins, boosts and settings) is saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes it.

What is sent, only if you say yes

The first time you play, the game asks "Help improve the game?". Only if you choose "Yes, share" does it send short gameplay summaries to help us tune the game: the level played, whether it was won, the time taken, shots and hits, how hearts were lost, the boosts used and what was done in the store, frame times, your device model, the game's version, the day the game was installed, and a random install number made by the game (not linked to your name or account). These go to Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies), which processes them for us. Test versions of the game also send the same summaries to a private Google Sheet (Google) that we read to tune the game.

Changing your mind

You can turn sharing off at any time in Settings. When you do, the game stops sending and asks Unity to delete the data it holds for your install.

Purchases and ads

This version of the game sells nothing and shows no ads. If that changes, this page will say so before it does.

Children

Sayak is made for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. The summaries above are shared only with Unity and Google, which process them for us.

Keeping data

Gameplay summaries are kept as long as they help us tune the game, and no longer than 24 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Contact

Zero Double Two, hello@zerodoubletwo.com. Write to us to ask what we hold or to have it deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date.
