Delete your Sayak data

Sayak, by Zero Double Two. Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Sayak has no accounts. It sends gameplay summaries only if you chose "Yes, share" when the game asked "Help improve the game?". Here is how to have them deleted.

In the game (quickest)

1. Open Sayak and tap the menu, then Settings.
2. Open Data & Playtest.
3. Tap Turn Off & Delete Cloud Data.

The game stops sending at once and asks Unity Analytics to delete everything it holds for your install.

By email

If you no longer have the game, write to hello@zerodoubletwo.com with the subject "Delete my Sayak data". Because we never know who you are, tell us your phone model and roughly when you played, so we can find your summaries. We delete them within 30 days and write back when it is done.

What is deleted

The gameplay summaries sent from your install: levels played, wins, times, shots and hits, hearts lost, boosts and store use, frame times, your device model, the game's version, the install date, the random install number and the country or region estimated from your internet address. They are deleted from Unity Analytics and, for test versions of the game, from our private Google Sheet.

What is kept

Nothing linked to your install. Your progress is saved only on your phone; uninstalling the game deletes it. Summaries that are never asked to be deleted are removed after 24 months.

See also the privacy policy.
